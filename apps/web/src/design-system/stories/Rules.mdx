# The eight rules

The Salvage Yard rules, as the components carry them.

1. **Every record is a tagged part.** A record id shown as data is a `Tag`, in its real case.
2. **Orange paint marks intent.** At most one `Button variant="primary"` and one
   `Plate variant="intent"` per view. Never orange for success.
3. **Blueprint is the to-be.** Drafted, inferred or proposed content is drawn (`variant="drawn"`,
   `Status` `draft` / `inferred`); applied content turns solid.
4. **Humans type, machines print.** Text a person wrote is a `Note`, with its author. Machine
   output never is.
5. **Grid ground, solid plates.** Content sits in a `Plate` on the ground (`AppShell` gives the
   ground). No texture under data.
6. **Square, no shadows.** No radius, no shadow; 1px lines, 2px for selected or focused.
7. **Expanded caps only for short labels.** Write sentence case; the CSS sets the caps of titles,
   labels, nav items and primary buttons (≤ 4–5 words).
8. **Shape and word on every status.** A `Status` with a word, from the one vocabulary.

## Never

- A hex value, `rgb()`, a font family, a font size, a radius or a shadow in a page or a component
  (lint refuses inline ones); a new `sy-` class outside the design package.
- Two primary buttons or two intent plates in one view; red for anything but failure.
- A `<select>` that navigates on change; text under 11px; animation other than the running pulse.
- Invented data in UI copy: show stored values exactly (Polish strings, ids, UTC timestamps).

## Voice

Counts first, then the next move: "171 / 400 steps · 113 queued. Resume the scan." Product nouns
stay plain (portal, scan, screen, process, record). Errors give the reference, the next move and
what still works. No emoji, no exclamation marks.
