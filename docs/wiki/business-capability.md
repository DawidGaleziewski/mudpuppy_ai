# Business capability

## What it is

A business capability is something an organisation can do, described by what it achieves rather than
how: "quote car insurance", "handle customer enquiries". Capabilities are stable; the processes,
screens and systems that deliver them change. Business capability analysis maps them to see what
the business offers, where it is strong or weak, and which parts of a system serve which capability.

## How Pathfinder uses it

In pass 2 the BA agent groups the screens it documented into `CAP` records: the things the portal
lets its users do. Each capability is linked to the screens and processes that deliver it
(`contains`). They give the high-level map of the documentation before the details, and they help a
rebuild team decide what to build first.

Example: CAP-001 "Product information and help content".
