The timing, in short

  1. 0–300 ms: the old page stays; only the nav's current item and the URL change. A fast load shows no loading state at all.
  2. At 300 ms: the new page's head (from the route) and its body as a skeleton at its final size, with the loader saying what is loading.
  3. Once shown, the skeleton stays at least 300 ms, so it never flashes.
  4. The data lands in the same boxes. Nothing below moves. Pictures arrive later in boxes that already have their size.
