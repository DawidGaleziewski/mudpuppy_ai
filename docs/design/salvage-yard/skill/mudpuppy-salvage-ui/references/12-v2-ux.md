# 12 · MudPuppy 2.0: UX design (answer to spec 039 · R-81)

What the console looks like and how it behaves once MudPuppy keeps **one living model of the portal**
and works out **what changed, when and why** after every scan. This answers the six asks in §8 of the
brief (`ux-requirements.md`, 2026-10-18) and its three open questions.

| You asked for (brief §8) | Where it is |
|---|---|
| 1 · Information architecture, and how time is chosen | §1, §2 |
| 2 · UI vocabulary | §3 |
| 3 · Wireframes for P1 pages and their states | §4 (screens `v2-01`…`v2-15`), §8 (states) |
| 4 · The review flow (keyboard, bulk, reasons, safe overturn) | §5 |
| 5 · The before / after view | §6 |
| 6 · The decision table, on screen and in the export | §7 |
| Open questions (global inbox, timeline on Home, map default) | §9 |

Everything is in the Salvage Yard system: the 2.0 screens reuse the v1 components and add eight
(`04-design-system.md` §3 "2.0"). Data on the screens is the real `reference-insurer` portal (screens, routes,
the three October scans, records) plus an **illustrative 2.0 fixture**: releases, later scans, changes, rules,
tests, cases. The fixture is consistent across all screens and written down in `13-v2-fixtures.md`, so it can
become the test fixture.

## 0. The design in six decisions

1. **"What changed" and "Rules" become sections; "Review" keeps its name and opens on "Needs you".**
   The nav is grouped: *Home · Changes · Review* (what to look at) · *Processes · Screens · Rules · Docs*
   (the portal model) · *Scans · Settings* (the machine). Map moves under Screens: it is the model, not a scan.
2. **Time is one control.** An **As of** picker sits next to the portal picker in the header (Now, a release,
   a scan). Model pages show the model as of that point; **Changes** compares a *from* (picked on the page) with
   the *to* (= As of). Nothing else has a date picker.
3. **Exceptions first, everything else one click away.** Each list opens on what needs a person and shows
   the count of what was explained automatically next to it, as a tab or a link. Nothing is hidden.
4. **Every decision is a sentence with a reason.** Confirm (reason optional), Reject and Overturn
   (reason required). The reason travels with the item and appears wherever the item appears,
   in the person's hand (manila typewriter note).
5. **Overturn is safe because it is visible and sticky.** An overturned explanation gets its own status,
   **Overturned** (manila ■), shows the reason everywhere, moves the item back to Needs you, and the
   automatic rule is told not to explain that change the same way again. Undo is one click; the audit is in
   *Review ▸ Decided*.
6. **Rules are decision tables with tests.** One row per rule outcome, inputs on the left, result on the
   right, a double rule between them, the boundary tests under the table, the history under the tests.
   The export is the same table in Markdown plus one plain sentence.

## 1. Information architecture

### 1.1 Navigation

| Group | Section | What it answers | Count in the nav | Screens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | **Home** | "How is this portal now?" Releases, last scan, counts, top of the queue | — | v2-01, v2-15 (All portals) |
| — | **Changes** *(new)* | "What changed since last time?" | changes in the current range | v2-02, v2-03 |
| — | **Review** *(reworked)* | "What needs me?" Needs you · Explained automatically · Decided | items that need you (orange) | v2-04, v2-05, v2-16, v2-14 |
| Portal model | **Processes** *(reworked)* | Proposed and accepted processes, how far explored | processes | v2-09, v2-10 |
| Portal model | **Screens** *(reworked)*, with **List · Map** | Screens with identity, variants and why, actions, history; the map of the portal now | active screens | v2-06, v2-07, v2-08 |
| Portal model | **Rules** *(new)* | Business rules as decision tables, tested | rules | v2-11, v2-12 |
| Portal model | **Docs** | The documentation records and export (unchanged in 2.0; PROC records gain rules) | — | — |
| Machine | **Scans** *(adjusted, P2)* | AI scans and **check runs**, each scan's own paths, Rebuild model | — | v1 `10-scans` + §10 |
| Machine | **Settings** *(adjusted, P2)* | Hints (release marker, A/B cookies, business refusals), check budgets | — | v1 `11-settings` + §10 |

Why these placements:
- **Changes is not inside Home.** It is the BA's first job after a release and needs filters, a range and a
  timeline. Home shows the counts and links into it.
- **"Needs you" is Review's default tab, not its own section.** Review already means "decide things" (v1 D-17).
  Splitting "Needs you" and "Explained automatically" into two sections would hide the second one, against
  "one click away". The nav count on Review is the Needs you count and is the only orange count in the nav.
- **Rules is a section** because the migration team reads rules across processes, and the brief makes them a
  first-class, tested object. Each process detail lists its rules and links here.
- **Map lives under Screens.** In 2.0 the map is the model's screens and links "now". A scan's own path stays
  reachable from the map ("One scan's path") and from Scans.
- The **assistant** stays a drawer on every page (`Ctrl J`). Its proposals land in the same queues
  (Review, Processes), marked "from the assistant" (UX-R90).

### 1.2 URLs and htmx

- Context is in the query string so every view is linkable: `?portal=reference-insurer&as_of=now`
  (or `release:9c21`, `scan:01a12f40-8c1e-7000-a2d9-4b7f1e6c3a05`), Changes adds `&from=prev-release` (or `date:2026-10-10`, `scan:01a110d5-2028-7000-8f3e-5c1a9b27d460`).
- **As of is a menu button, not a select that navigates on change** (WCAG 3.2.2): it opens a menu of links, newest first
  (Now · Scan 01a1340c-3b97 · 9c21 · Scan 01a12f40-8c1e · 3e1a · older scans and releases); choosing one loads the page with `as_of` via
  `hx-get` + `hx-push-url`. All model pages read the same parameter.
- Long lists (Changes, Review, Screens) load in pages of 50 with `hx-get` on "Show more" and keep the counts
  at the top. Review actions post to commands and swap the item with `hx-swap="outerHTML"`; the result is
  announced in a polite live region (`role="status"`), failures in `role="alert"`.

### 1.3 Moving around (2.3, `20-navigation-and-canvases/`)

- **Came from** (T-01): a page opened by an in-app link from a page the crumbs don't lead back to shows
  "← {Kind} {title}" above the crumbs, back to the exact address. One step.
- **Previous / Next** (T-02): on a record page opened from a list, a pager in the head's actions; J / K.
- **All portals** (T-12): every section shown; the portal sections disabled with "Pick a portal to open these.";
  first visit with one portal opens it.
- **Header** (T-11): wordmark · Portal [portal ▾][store ▾] · As of · Search · Ask (with the assistant's status) · ?
- **Loading** (T-10, T-14): the head at once; a skeleton of the final size with the loader only after 300 ms; nothing on
  screen moves when content arrives (CLS ≤ 0.05).

## 2. Time

| Control | Where | Values | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **As of** | Header, next to Portal, on every page (a menu button) | Now (default) · each release per part (`9c21 · kalkulator · 16 Oct`) · each scan | Model pages (Screens, Map, Processes, Rules, Docs) show the model as it was. A banner says so and offers "Back to now". Writes are disabled while not Now. |
| **From** | Changes page only | Previous release of each part (default) · a date · a scan | Changes lists what differs between *from* and *to* (= As of). |
| **Range bracket** | Release timeline on Changes | — | Shows the chosen range over the releases and scans; clicking a release sets *from*. |

- **Releases are per portal part.** The shell and each embedded app have their own lane (UX-R14). A
  gradual rollout is a dashed span ("5f08 + 9c21 served together") between the old and new release.
- **Scans** sit on their own lane under the releases: AI scans as filled squares, check runs as diamonds.
- A portal **without release markers** has one lane drawn dashed: "release unknown · compared page by page"
  (UX-R02), and Home and Changes say changes are detected more slowly.
- **Needs you is not time-bound**: every item shows its age and stays until decided. **Explained automatically** follows the
  Changes range (default: since the previous release; "all time" is one click away). **Decided** is the full history.
- **Release watch:** releases are known between scans because MudPuppy fetches each part's release marker every 30 minutes
  (no crawling, no AI). That is how "9c21 from 16 Oct 14:02Z" and a rollout's end are known; screens and variants are still
  only seen by scans.

## 3. UI vocabulary

The model's words stay internal. These are the words on screen, in the export and in the assistant's replies.

| Internal (design.md §3) | UI word | Definition shown in the glossary | Example on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| abstract state | **screen** | A page or step of the portal, kept as one thing across scans. Active, Merged into…, Retired. | "Kalkulator OC/AC — dane kierowcy · Active" |
| screen variant | **variant** (r1, r2…) | One way the screen looked. Every variant says **why** it differs: Release · Persona · Error · A/B test · Data · Unknown. | "r3 · Persona premium" |
| transition to another screen | **link** | Something you click that leads to another screen. Active · Not seen since 〈date〉 · Not checked on 〈release〉 · Retired · Replaced → 〈new〉. | "Not seen since 17 Oct" |
| action inside a screen | **action** | Something on the screen that doesn't leave it: a dialog, a field, a validation. Drawn inside the screen, never as a map line. | "6 actions inside" |
| deviation / diff | **change** | A difference between two points in time. | "New action · Assistance 24h" |
| claim / hypothesis | **finding** + **explanation** | A finding is what the system noticed; the explanation is why it thinks it happened. | "Banner on 6 of 11 visits" · "A/B test?" |
| auto-resolved | **Explained automatically** | The system explained it with a rule. You can overturn it. | "Explained as a new release because the app's code changed at 14:02" |
| human required | **Needs you** | The system couldn't decide, or the change affects documentation. | "Needs you · 24" |
| human override | **Overturned** | A person replaced an automatic explanation. Their reason shows everywhere. | "Overturned · you · 17 Oct · Not flaky: it happens every time with +48." |
| build / build epoch | **release** | A version of a portal part, named by its marker. | "Release 9c21" |
| — | **portal part** | The shell or an embedded app with its own releases. | "Shell", "Kalkulator app" |
| gradual rollout | **rollout** | Old and new release served at the same time. | "Rollout · 16 Oct 14:02 – 17 Oct 09:30" |
| journey | **path** | The steps one case or one scan took. | "dane kierowcy → opcje → wynik" |
| case | **case** | One run through a process with given inputs. | "Case 7 · age 20" |
| coverage (process) | **explored** | How far a process was tried on a release: Thorough · Partial · Not explored. | "Explored on 9c21: thorough" |
| coverage (screen) | **checked** | Actions on the screen tried on the current release. | "Checked 8 / 11 on 9c21" |
| marker polling | **release watch** | Checking each part's release marker every 30 min, without a scan. | "Seen by release watch at 14:02Z" |
| check run | **check run** | Replays and tests, no AI. vs **AI scan**. | "Check run 01a1340c-3b97 · 64 checks" |
| decision table | **decision table**, one **rule row** per outcome | | |
| boundary test | **boundary test** | Inputs just below, at and above a threshold. | "Tested at 20, 21, 22: pass" |
| confidence levels | **Observed · Inferred · Needs you · Confirmed · Overturned** | Always shown as a status (shape + word). | |
| model rebuild | **Rebuild model** | Recompute screens, links and rules from all scans. | |

Words we avoid on screen: abstract state, journey, hypothesis, deviation, epoch, claim, verdict (v1's
reconciliation said "verdict"; 2.0 says **explanation**).

What each page does, beyond the brief's list:
- **Home** opens on the release strip and four counts (changes, needs you, processes explored, rules broken or
  changed); the Needs you plate (the view's one intent plate) shows the top 5. The assistant moves to the side
  column with suggestion chips ("What changed since 3e1a?").
- **Changes** is grouped by process, then screen. A change row: the change in one sentence · kind · explanation ·

  **suggestion** on it.
- **Rules** lists every rule with where it is decided, its status (Proposed · Tested · Confirmed · Broken ·
  Changed · Replaced) and its last test.

## 5. The review flow

### 5.1 Anatomy of the queue (v2-04)

- **Tabs:** *Needs you* (default) · *Explained automatically* · *Decided* (everything a person decided, newest first,
  with reasons: the audit trail).
- **Filters** with counts: kind (Findings · Rules · Removals · Processes · Same screen? · Records), portal part,
  process, age. Counts update with the filters.
- **Queue** (left): grouped by kind; each item is one line of finding, a kind chip, an evidence hint and its age.
  Similar items form a **group** with one checkbox and one action ("Confirm 12").
- **Detail** (right): the finding as a heading · confidence · evidence (screenshot regions, visits, release,
  persona) · tests run and results · the proposed explanation · the decision bar (Confirm · Reject · Comment) with
  the reason field · "What happens if you confirm".

### 5.2 Keyboard

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `J` / `K` | Next / previous item (or group) |
| `Enter` | Open the item's detail (focus moves to the heading) |
| `X` | Select the item or group for bulk |
| `C` | Confirm: focus moves to the reason field (optional) with Confirm as the action; `Enter` saves, `Esc` cancels |
| `R` | Reject: focus moves to the reason field (required); `Enter` saves, `Esc` cancels |
| `M` | Comment |
| `O` | Overturn (on *Explained automatically*) |
| `Shift C` | Confirm the selection |
| `U` | Undo the last decision (also in the toast) |
| `/` | Filter |
| `?` | All shortcuts |

After a decision, focus moves to the next item and the outcome is announced ("Confirmed. 23 left.").
A key bar (sticky in the app) at the bottom of the queue shows at most five keys; `?` lists the rest.

**Single-key shortcuts (WCAG 2.1.4):** they work only while focus is in the queue or the detail, never while typing in a field,
and Settings ▸ Keyboard can turn them off or remap them. Every shortcut has a visible button that does the same.

### 5.3 Reasons

- One line, 200 characters. Required for Reject and Overturn, optional for Confirm and Comment.
- The field remembers the last three reasons for the same kind as suggestions (for bulk work).
- Wherever a decided item appears later (Changes, Screens, Map inspector, Processes, Rules, export), its reason
  is shown as a manila note with who and when.

### 5.4 Bulk

- Groups are formed by the system (same kind + same explanation + same release, e.g. "12 links removed with
  release 3e1a"). Selecting a group selects all its items; the action button says the count ("Confirm 12").
- Bulk Reject and bulk Overturn ask for one reason, applied to all.
- A bulk action is one command; it succeeds or fails as a whole, and Undo reverts the whole group.

### 5.5 Linked decisions

Some items are about the same thing seen three ways, e.g. the banner on wynik: the **A/B test?** finding, the **Same screen?**
conflict (Marek: different screens) and the **process suggestion** (r3 as a variant of step 3). Each one's detail lists the others
under **Also settles**. One decision closes the bundle: confirming the A/B test settles the conflict as "same screen, variants"
(your reason shown next to Marek's) and applies the suggestion; settling Same screen? as "different screens" first turns the finding
into "New screen on 6 of 11 visits". A process that a person edited only ever gets suggestions, and those are decided in Review.

### 5.6 Overturning safely (v2-05)

- **Where:** every row in *Explained automatically*, and every place an automatic explanation is shown
  (Changes, Screen detail variants, Map inspector), has **Overturn**.
- **The dialog** shows what the system said and the rule it used, asks what it is instead (a short list +
  Other), and a required reason. It says what will happen: the item moves to Needs you, it is marked
  Overturned everywhere, and the automatic rule won't explain this change the same way again.
- **Visible:** status **Overturned** (manila ■ = a person's hand) + the reason note, in every place the item shows.
  Counts on Home and Changes say "1 overturned".
- **Sticky:** later scans and model rebuilds keep the person's explanation. If new evidence contradicts it, the
  system raises a new finding ("Your explanation 'not flaky' disagrees with 3 passing rechecks"); it never
  flips the decision itself.
- **Reversible:** Undo overturn (with the reason kept in the history). *Decided* (v2-16) lists every overturn.

### 5.7 The Decided tab (v2-16)

Every decision a person made, newest first: when, who, what, the item, the reason, and Undo while it's still possible. Filters by
person and kind, and "Overturns only". It is the audit trail for overturns and the place to answer "who decided this, and why?".

### 5.8 Failed writes

Writes go through commands. The button shows "Saving…" (`aria-busy`), then either a toast ("Confirmed · Undo")
or an inline `role="alert"` callout with the command's message, what was not saved, **Reload and try again**, and the reason the
person typed kept in the field (states screen, state 8).

## 6. The before / after view (v2-03)

- **Two sides, labelled by what differs**, not by "left/right": "Without banner · r2" / "With banner · r3",
  each with the visit, time and release.
- **The changed region** is outlined on the side that has it (the system pointing: `.sy-mark--overlay`, label
  "Changed"), and marked "Not here" at the same place on the other side, so the eye can find "where it isn't":
  a dashed box for things that float, a dashed line where an inserted region pushed the page (`.sy-mark--gone`,
  `19-console-answers/05`).
- **Three modes:** Side by side (default) · Overlay (the after drawn over the before at 50%, for small shifts;
  `.sy-compare--overlay` with a slider) ·
  Wireframe (both sides as boxes and labels, for pages with personal data or heavy imagery).
- **Context diff** under the pictures: Release, Persona, Inputs, Cookies, Flags, Time. Rows that differ are
  marked (`.sy-differs`, the word "differs"); same rows say "same". This is where the explanation comes from,
  so it sits right above the explanation.
- **Explanation and tests** (drawn, because they are the system's inference), then the decision bar.

## 7. The decision table (v2-12)

### 7.1 On screen

```
BR-021 · Driver must be 21 or older                      Changed · Needs you
Where decided: Kalkulator OC/AC — dane kierowcy · "Dalej" · process Oblicz składkę OC/AC

            INPUTS              ║  RESULT
 #   Wiek kierowcy              ║  Next screen                         Message
 1   < 21                       ║  Nie możemy Cię ubezpieczyć (/kalkulator/odmowa)
 2   ≥ 21                       ║  Kalkulator OC/AC — opcje
Wiek kierowcy = age on today's date, from "Data urodzenia". First matching row wins.
```

- **Inputs left, result right, a double rule between** (`.sy-dt-result`). One row per outcome; conditions in mono
  (`< 21`, `≥ 21`, `any`); results use the portal's Polish screen names and messages.
- **Provenance above the table** (`.sy-facts--table`, outside the caption; `19-console-answers/07`): where each input
  comes from, and the hit policy in plain words.
- **Hidden data:** when the outcome differs for identical visible inputs, the table gets a row
  "*any* · *any* → depends on data not visible from the UI" and the rule's status says **Decided on data not
  visible from the UI** (BR-024).
- **Boundary tests** sit under the table: inputs just below, at and above each threshold, expected vs got,
  pass/fail, per release. **Rerun history** below that, including the run that disagreed with the old table.
- **Changes:** the previous table, struck through, with "Threshold moved from 18 to 21 with release 9c21".
- **Decision:** Confirm · Reject (reason) · **Reword** (the sentence that goes into the documentation).

### 7.2 In the export

Developers and testers read the export more than the console. Every rule exports in the same shape (this is the preview on
`v2-12-rule-detail.html`):

```markdown
## BR-021 · Driver must be 21 or older

If the driver is under 21 on the day of the quote, "Dalej" on Kalkulator OC/AC — dane kierowcy
leads to the exit page Nie możemy Cię ubezpieczyć; from 21 it leads to Kalkulator OC/AC — opcje.

- Where:      Kalkulator OC/AC — dane kierowcy (/kalkulator/kierowca, step 1), button "Dalej"
- Process:    Oblicz składkę OC/AC (PROC-001), step 1
- Input:      Wiek kierowcy = age on today's date, from field "Data urodzenia"
- Hit policy: first match (rows are checked in order; the first that matches decides)

| # | Wiek kierowcy | → Next screen                                           | Message |
|---|---------------|---------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| 1 | < 21          | Nie możemy Cię ubezpieczyć (/kalkulator/odmowa)         | (none)  |
| 2 | ≥ 21          | Kalkulator OC/AC — opcje (/kalkulator/kierowca, step 2) | (none)  |

- Tested:   2026-10-17 · release 9c21 · check run 01a1340c-3b97 · 20 → row 1, 21 → row 2, 22 → row 2: pass
- Changed:  release 9c21 (2026-10-16): threshold 18 → 21. Before: < 18 → exit page; ≥ 18 → opcje
- Evidence: check run 01a1340c-3b97, case 7: age 20 → Nie możemy Cię ubezpieczyć
- Status:   changed, not confirmed yet (needs a person)
```

Rules: the sentence first (the reworded one if a person reworded it), then where, process, input provenance and hit policy,
the table, then tests, changes, evidence and status. Empty cells are "(none)"; dates in the export are ISO (`2026-10-17`).
A CSV of all rules (`rule, row, input, condition, result, tested_on, release, status`) goes with it for test generation.

## 8. States (brief §6)

`v2-13-states.html` shows each state on the page where it matters most, with a page × state matrix:

| State | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Empty (new portal) | `.sy-state` "Yard's empty" with the three first steps; the next one marked Next |
| First scan running | Running meter + live counts; "Changes and Needs you start after the second scan" |
| Model being rebuilt | Status banner with %; pages show the previous model; decisions are kept and re-applied; a summary when done |
| Nothing needs you | `.sy-state` "Nothing needs you" + link to Explained automatically (reviewing is optional) |
| 500+ items | Groups of similar items first, bulk actions, 50 per page, counts fixed at the top |
| No release markers | Warning on Home and Changes, a dashed "release unknown" lane, a link to Settings ▸ Hints |
| Conflict (two labels contradict) | Intent callout with both answers and the person's reason; docs mark the step "in dispute" until decided |
| Failed write | Inline alert with the command's message; nothing saved; the typed reason kept; Reload and try again |
| Also: viewing the past | Banner "As of release 3e1a" with Back to now; writes disabled |
| Loading (2.3) | Nothing under 300 ms; then the page's skeleton at its final size with `.sy-loader` naming what loads; a refetching list keeps its rows dimmed (`aria-busy`). `v2-23`, `v2-24` |

## 9. Open questions from the brief: our answers

1. **Should "Needs you" be a global inbox across portals?** Default **per portal**, with **All portals** in the
   portal picker turning Review into one inbox with a Portal column (v2-15). Decisions need the portal's context
   (releases, personas, rules), and the BA works one portal per session; but a BA who looks after several portals
   needs one place to see where work waits. Home ▸ All portals shows the per-portal counts.
2. **How much of the release timeline belongs on Home?** **A compact strip:** the latest release per part, the
   last scan, and a 2-week mini timeline. The full timeline (with the range bracket) lives on Changes, one click
   away from the strip.
3. **Should the map default to "now" or "this release vs previous"?** **Now.** It's the map people navigate by.
   Arriving from Changes (or picking "Changes since 3e1a · 9c21") opens the release overlay. The view is in the
   URL, so a link from a change opens the map in the right mode.

## 10. P2 pages (notes, not drawn)

- **Scans (UX-R70–72):** two kinds in the history table, "AI scan" and "Check run" (diamond icon, "64 checks ·
  5 findings"). A scan's paths as a tree (backtracks branch), each step with a result chip: ok · validation ·
  business error · technical error · timeout · blocked. **Rebuild model** in the page head: progress banner, then
  the summary from state 3.
- **Settings (UX-R80–81):** a **Hints** section per portal: release marker (where it shows, with an example and a
  "test it" button), A/B cookies (name + example values), business refusals (message text, e.g. "Nie możemy Cię
  ubezpieczyć"), each with a one-line explanation of what it changes. **Budgets**: checks per scan, probes per
  form step, minutes per check run.
- **Assistant (UX-R90):** answers "What changed?" with the Changes summary and a link; can start a rebuild or a
  check run (confirm first); its proposals carry "from the assistant" and land in Review or Processes.
